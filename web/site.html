KSCDR Hackathon · working prototype

A white cane that sees what is ahead and how far.

A camera and an AI accelerator on the cane name 152 kinds of object with their direction and distance. Two time-of-flight sensors catch obstacles and drop-offs, and a vibration motor reacts within a second of power-on, whether the computer is up or not.

What it does

Working

Names objects, with direction and distance

YOLO11s on the cane's Hailo-8L. Straight ahead, the forward ToF sensor gives the measured distance. Elsewhere the camera estimates it from the object's size.

"chair ahead, 1.3 meters"
Working

Vibrates before you touch the obstacle

An ESP32 reads the forward sensor 30 times a second and pulses the motor faster as you get closer. Silent beyond 1.5 m, solid under 0.4 m.

felt, not heard
Tuning on walks

Warns of drops and kerbs

A second sensor watches the ground about 50 cm past the tip and alarms on a sudden change: one long pulse and a spoken warning.

"Careful, drop ahead"
Working

One-button assistant

Press: Gemini describes the scene, then you can ask a question, such as "read the sign". Hold: it reads text. Offline, the same press uses the cane's own detector and Tesseract OCR.

press · hold
Working

Never silent about the unknown

Anything the model is unsure of, or never learned, is still announced as "obstacle" with its direction and distance.

"obstacle left, about 2 meters"
Working

Says when it is broken

A dead camera, a lost sensor link, a sensor that cannot see the ground: each one is spoken, because a silent failure looks like safety.

"Ground sensor not working"

Live dashboard for judges

The cane serves its own dashboard: camera with real boxes, every object's bearing and distance, the ToF reading beside the camera's estimate for whatever is straight ahead, the ground sensor, and everything the cane says, updated four times a second.

  1. The cane serves it

    The dashboard runs on the cane all the time, locked with a private token. It draws frames only while someone is watching, so it costs no battery otherwise.

  2. One command opens a secure tunnel

    bash ~/smartcane/tools/go_live.sh gives the cane a public https address through Cloudflare and waits until it answers.

  3. Judges open the link

    On any phone or laptop, directly or through this website. Ctrl-C closes the tunnel and the cane is private again.

The dashboard shows the camera, so it never runs without a token, and the tunnel closes when the demo ends. If the venue's internet fails, a recording made with tools/record_demo.py plays back on the same page. Full steps: docs/deployment.md.

Two computers with separate jobs. The ESP32 owns the reflexes, the Raspberry Pi owns the understanding.
